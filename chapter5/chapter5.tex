\section{Boltzmann Distribution}\label{sec:boltzmann_distribution} % (fold)
In this chapter, we will learn how to apply some rules of probability and statistics to calculate the probability that a particle is in any given microstate. For example, inside a gas container, we can calculate the probability of finding particles moving with velocity $v_{1}$, or any other states. Firstly, let's derive the probability density function:
\begin{figure}[H]
	\begin{center}
		\includegraphics[width=0.6\textwidth]{~/PhyAI/chapter5/5_1.png}
	\end{center}
	\caption{An imaginary experiment}\label{fig:5_1}
\end{figure}
Consider a system divided into two parts. The first part is the single particle found in a microstate $s$, and the second part is the rest of the container (or what I call the reservoir or environment). For any microstates $s_{1}$ and $s_{2}$, we have:
\begin{equation*}
	\begin{split}
		\frac{\mathcal{P}(s_{1})}{\mathcal{P}(s_{2})}&=\frac{\Omega_{R}(s_{1})}{\Omega_{R}(s_{2})}=\exp{\left(\frac{S_{R}(s_{1})-S_{R}(s_{2})}{k_{B}}\right)}
	\end{split}
\end{equation*}
From the thermodynamics identity (Theorem ~\ref{The Thermodynamics Identity}):
$$dS=\frac{dU }{T}\quad\text{($V$ and $N$ are held as constants)}$$
For abbreviation, I denote: $$ \beta=\frac{1 }{k_{B}T} $$
We obtain:
\begin{equation*}
	\begin{split}
		\frac{\mathcal{P}(s_{1})}{\mathcal{P}(s_{2})}&=\exp{(\beta(U_{R}(s_{1})-U_{R}(s_{2})))}\\
		&=\exp(-\beta(E(s_{1})-E(s_{2})))\\
		\Leftrightarrow \frac{\mathcal{P}(s_{1}) }{\mathcal{P}(s_{2})}&=\frac{\exp{(-\beta E(s_{1}))}}{\exp(-\beta E(s_{2}))}
	\end{split}
\end{equation*}
Finally for any microstate $s$, we always have the proportional relationship:
$$ \mathcal{P}(s)\propto e^{-\beta E(s)} $$
The probability density $\mathcal{P}(s)$ is formed by normalizing function $Z$ as follows:
$$ Z=\sum_{s}e^{-\beta E(s)}\Rightarrow \mathcal{P}(s)=\frac{1 }{Z}e^{-\beta E(s)} $$
$Z$ is the \textbf{partition function}, and exponential factor $e^{-\beta E(s)}$ is called the \textbf{Boltzmann factor}.
\begin{definition}[Boltzmann Distribution]
	\label{Boltzmann Distribution}
	The probability of finding particle in any microstate $s$ is:
	$$ \mathcal{P}(s)=\frac{1 }{Z}e^{-\beta E(s)} $$
	with $Z$ is the \textbf{partition function}: $$ Z=\sum_{s}e^{-\beta E(s)} $$
\end{definition}
\begin{corollary}[Average Energy]
	\label{Average Energy}
	The average energy of any system is:
	$$ \overline{E}=-\frac{\partial \ln{Z} }{\partial \beta} $$
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
	\begin{equation*}
		\begin{split}
			\overline{E}&=\sum_{s}E(s)\mathcal{P}(s)=\frac{1 }{Z}\sum_{s}E(s)e^{-\beta E(s)}=-\frac{1 }{Z}\frac{\partial Z }{\partial \beta}=-\frac{\partial \ln{Z} }{\partial \beta}
		\end{split}
	\end{equation*}
\end{proof}
